1. What we do

We send PDF documents you upload to a fax number you provide. We do not offer fax receiving at this time.

2. Prices and payment

  • The price of each fax is calculated from its page count and shown to you before you pay. The current prices are on the pricing page.

3. Delivery and retries

If a fax cannot be delivered at the first attempt (for example because the line is busy or does not answer), we retry automatically, up to four attempts in total, with increasing intervals. We email you when the fax is delivered. If we are certain it cannot be delivered (for example the number is not a fax line, or every attempt has failed), we email you once so that you can check the number and send it again.

4. No refunds on single faxes

Single (pay-per-fax) faxes are not refundable. Each attempt uses a paid telephone line. This includes faxes that cannot be delivered because the number you entered was wrong or is not a fax machine. By paying you expressly request that we begin the service immediately and acknowledge that the right of withdrawal ends once the fax has been sent.
