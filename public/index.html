Questions

Do I need a fax machine?

No. Upload a PDF from your phone or computer and we send it to any fax number in the countries we serve.

How much does it cost?

A fax of up to 3 A4 pages costs €1.50, and your very first fax is €1. Longer documents cost a little more per extra page, and you always see the exact price before you pay.

What happens if the line is busy?

We retry automatically with increasing gaps between attempts. You get one email when it is delivered, or one email if it definitely cannot be delivered, so you can check the number and send again.

Is there a subscription?

Only if you want one. The monthly plan is optional, and cancelling is a single button under “Your Subscription” in your account. No phone calls, no retention offers.

Can I get a refund?

Single faxes are not refundable once sent, because the phone line has been used. The full rules are in our Terms.
