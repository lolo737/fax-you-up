Privacy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The controller is Eriu Technology Limited, Skerries, Co. Dublin, Ireland (CRO no. 817401). Contact: hello@faxyouup.example.

What we collect and why

  • Your document and the destination number: to send the fax (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your email address: to tell you whether the fax was delivered, to send sign-in links, and to apply the first-fax price once (contract).
  • Payment data: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep the amount, date and Stripe reference for our accounts (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Delivery logs: status, attempts and failure reasons, to retry and to support you (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it for us

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Telnyx (fax transmission)
  • Netlify (hosting and file storage)
  • Our transactional email provider (delivery and sign-in emails)

Some of these providers process data outside the EEA under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

We set one strictly necessary cookie when you sign in (to keep you signed in) and remember your language choice in your browser. No advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party fonts.

How long we keep it

Documents and delivery logs are kept while you have an account so you can download them. Payment records are kept for as long as Irish tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and object to processing, by emailing us. You can complain to the Data Protection Commission (Ireland) or your local supervisory authority.
